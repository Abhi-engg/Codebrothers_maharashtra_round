# Re:Learn — Phase 1: Dataset Architecture & Engineering Specification

---

## 1. Dataset Philosophy & Educational Grounding

In standard automated grading systems, student submissions are evaluated against a binary key ($y \in \{0, 1\}$). If a student computes $v = +60\text{ cm}$ instead of $v = -60\text{ cm}$, a traditional test platform marks it incorrect and presents the standard answer.

**Re:Learn operates on the principles of Physics Education Research (PER):**
1. **Misconceptions are systematic, not random:** Wrong answers are generated by internally coherent but physically incorrect mental models (e.g., Aristotelian impetus, absolute current consumption, or unilateral ray propagation).
2. **Identical numbers can stem from disparate root causes:** Two students can arrive at identical incorrect numerical values through fundamentally different mathematical or conceptual missteps.
3. **Working steps provide ground truth:** Without intermediate mathematical substitutions, sign designations, or verbal reasoning, distinguishing an arithmetic slip from a deep conceptual error is statistically unidentifiable.
4. **Temporal consistency reveals cognitive traps:** A single mistake may be an isolated lapse, but recurring error transitions across related questions signify persistent misconception patterns.

## 3. Formal Misconception Taxonomy

Each misconception is registered with a standardized identifier, trigger pattern, differentiation criteria, and curriculum mapping.

### 3.1 Grade 9: Mechanics & Gravitation
* **`MISC-G09-MOT-01` — Speed vs. Velocity / Distance vs. Displacement Conflation**
  * *Description:* Student ignores directional vectors in closed paths, equating total displacement to total distance traveled.
  * *Trigger Pattern:* Calculates average velocity as $\frac{\text{total path length}}{\Delta t}$ on a return journey ($v_{\text{avg}} \neq 0$).
* **`MISC-G09-MOT-02` — Impetus Fallacy (Force Sustains Motion)**
  * *Description:* Believes an object moving at constant velocity must have a net non-zero external force acting in the direction of motion.
  * *Trigger Pattern:* Claims $F_{\text{net}} > 0$ when $a = 0$ on frictionless surfaces.
* **`MISC-G09-GRV-01` — Mass-Proportional Gravitational Acceleration**
  * *Description:* Believes heavier masses experience greater gravitational acceleration in free fall ($a \propto m$).
  * *Trigger Pattern:* Predicts a $10\text{ kg}$ sphere reaches ground earlier than a $1\text{ kg}$ sphere from the same height in vacuum.

### 3.2 Grade 10: Optics & Electricity
* **`MISC-G10-OPT-01` — Inversion of Cartesian Sign Convention for Focal Length**
  * *Description:* Treats focal length of converging concave mirrors as positive ($f > 0$) or forgets that incident direction sets the positive axis.
  * *Trigger Pattern:* Substitutes $f = +15\text{ cm}$ instead of $f = -15\text{ cm}$ into mirror formula.
* **`MISC-G10-OPT-02` — Conflation of Mirror and Lens Formulas**
  * *Description:* Uses the lens formula $\frac{1}{v} - \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{f}$ for spherical mirror calculations.
  * *Trigger Pattern:* Subtracts $\frac{1}{u}$ from $\frac{1}{v}$ for a mirror problem.
* **`MISC-G10-OPT-03` — Universal Inversion Fallacy for Concave Mirrors**
  * *Description:* Believes concave mirrors can *only* produce real, inverted images, ignoring virtual upright images formed when $u < f$.
  * *Trigger Pattern:* Assumes image must be real when object is placed between Focus $F$ and Pole $P$.
* **`MISC-G10-ELE-01` — Current Consumption Fallacy in Series Circuits**
  * *Description:* Believes electric current decreases ("gets used up") sequentially across resistors in a single closed loop.
  * *Trigger Pattern:* States $I_2 < I_1$ after passing through resistor $R_1$.
* **`MISC-G10-ELE-02` — Parallel Branch Current Invariance**
  * *Description:* Believes current divides equally among parallel branches regardless of differing branch resistances.
  * *Trigger Pattern:* Sets $I_1 = I_2 = \frac{I_{\text{total}}}{2}$ when $R_1 \neq R_2$.
* **`MISC-G10-ELE-03` — Parallel Equivalent Resistance Reciprocal Omission**
  * *Description:* Computes $\frac{1}{R_p} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2}$ and records the decimal sum as the final resistance without inverting.
  * *Trigger Pattern:* Writes $R_p = 0.25\,\Omega$ instead of $R_p = 4\,\Omega$ for two $8\,\Omega$ resistors.

### 3.3 Grade 11: Classical Mechanics & Dynamics
* **`MISC-G11-KIN-01` — Zero-Velocity Implies Zero-Acceleration at Trajectory Apex**
  * *Description:* Assumes that because instantaneous vertical velocity is zero at maximum height ($v_y = 0$), vertical acceleration must also be zero ($a_y = 0$).
  * *Trigger Pattern:* Claims net force or acceleration is zero at highest point of projectile flight.
* **`MISC-G11-DYN-01` — Normal Force Is Always Equal to $mg$**
  * *Description:* Equates normal reaction force $N$ universally to $mg$, failing to resolve forces along the inclined plane or account for vertical external forces.
  * *Trigger Pattern:* Sets $N = mg$ on an incline of angle $\theta$, rather than $N = mg \cos\theta$.
* **`MISC-G11-ROT-01` — Radial Invariance of Tangential Velocity**
  * *Description:* Assumes all points on a rigid rotating body possess the same linear speed, confusing angular velocity $\omega$ with tangential velocity $v = \omega r$.
  * *Trigger Pattern:* Assumes a point on the rim moves at identical linear speed to a point near the axle.

### 3.4 Grade 12: Electromagnetism & Modern Physics
* **`MISC-G12-EST-01` — Zero Potential Implies Zero Electric Field**
  * *Description:* Conflates scalar potential $V$ with vector field $E$, believing $V=0 \implies E=0$ (ignoring $E = -\nabla V$).
  * *Trigger Pattern:* States electric field is zero at the midpoint between equal and opposite charges (dipole center).
* **`MISC-G12-EMI-01` — Lenz's Law Opposes Field Rather Than Flux Change**
  * *Description:* Believes induced magnetic field always opposes the external field direction, rather than opposing the *time rate of change* of magnetic flux ($\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$).
  * *Trigger Pattern:* Predicts opposite induced field even when an external magnetic field is decreasing in magnitude.

### 3.5 Non-Misconception Baseline Classes
To prevent false-positive misconception attribution, every input must also be testable against standard baseline categories:
* **`CORRECT`**: Correct physical formulation, valid sign substitution, correct calculation.
* **`SLIP-ARITHMETIC`**: Conceptually and mathematically correct formulation, but arithmetic computation slip (e.g., $15 \times 4 = 55$).
* **`SLIP-UNIT`**: Correct equation and signs, but failure to convert units (e.g., entering $15\text{ cm}$ without converting to SI meters when using $c = 3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$).
* **`UNCERTAIN-GUESS`**: Bare number provided without working, contradictory scribbles, or random guessing where evidence is statistically insufficient.

## 5. Disambiguation Strategy: Distinguishing Identical Wrong Answers

A key requirement in the Re:Learn problem statement is:
> **"Distinguish different misconceptions that produce similar wrong answers."**

Below are concrete, testable cases embedded in our dataset:

### Case Study 1: Concave Mirror Image Distance
* **Question:** $u = -20\text{ cm}$, $f = -15\text{ cm}$. Find $v$.
* **Expected Correct Solution:**
  $$\frac{1}{v} = \frac{1}{f} - \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{-15} - \frac{1}{-20} = -\frac{1}{15} + \frac{1}{20} = -\frac{1}{60} \implies v = -60\text{ cm}$$

| Student | Answer | Written Working Steps | Model 1 Ground Truth |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Student A** | $+60\text{ cm}$ | `1/v = 1/f - 1/u = 1/15 - 1/20 = 1/60 => v = +60` | `MISC-G10-OPT-01` (Sign Convention Error: treated $f$ as $+15$) |
| **Student B** | $+60\text{ cm}$ | `1/v = 1/f + 1/u = 1/(-15) + 1/(-20) = -7/60` then drops negative | `SLIP-ARITHMETIC` (Arithmetic Drop of sign) |
| **Student C** | $+60\text{ cm}$ | Bare answer entered without working | `UNCERTAIN-GUESS` (Abstain: prompt for steps) |

### Case Study 2: Series Resistors and Circuit Current
* **Question:** A $6\text{V}$ battery is connected across $R_1 = 2\,\Omega$ and $R_2 = 4\,\Omega$ in series. What is the current through $R_2$?
* **Expected Correct Solution:**
  $$R_{\text{eq}} = 2 + 4 = 6\,\Omega \implies I = \frac{V}{R} = \frac{6\text{V}}{6\,\Omega} = 1\text{A}$$

| Student | Answer | Written Working Steps | Model 1 Ground Truth |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Student D** | $1.5\text{ A}$ | `I = V / R2 = 6 / 4 = 1.5 A` | `MISC-G10-ELE-02` (Local Voltage Fallacy: applies total $6\text{V}$ across $R_2$) |
| **Student E** | $1.5\text{ A}$ | `Req = (2*4)/(2+4) = 8/6 = 1.33 => I = 6/1.33` rounded | `MISC-G10-ELE-03` (Series/Parallel Swap: used parallel formula) |

## 6. Senior ML/DL Training Pipeline Safeguards

### 6.1 Leakage-Free Stratified Group Splitting
To prevent near-duplicate leakage:
* Every question instance has a `template_group_id` (e.g. `GRP-G10-MIRROR-REAL`).
* All numerical perturbations, sign variations, and student response styles sharing that template are assigned **strictly to either the Train, Validation, or Test set**.
* **Split Ratio:** $70\%$ Train, $15\%$ Validation, $15\%$ Held-out Test.

### 6.2 Data Quality & Annotation Verification Checklist
1. **Physical Soundness:** Every question has an unambiguous, physically verified solution.
2. **Deterministic Step Verification:** Calculations in `expected_working_steps` are verifiable by computer algebra (SymPy).
3. **Plausibility Check:** Student wrong answers must be derived from genuine conceptual deviations, not randomly sampled uniform noise.
4. **Source Attribution:** Each entry specifies origin (`NCERT_Standard`, `PER_Literature`, `Authored_Pedagogical_Variant`).
