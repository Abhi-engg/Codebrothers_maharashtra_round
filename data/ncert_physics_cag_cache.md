## GRADE 9 PHYSICS

### Chapter 8 & 9: Motion, Force, and Laws of Motion
*   **Distance vs. Displacement:**
    *   Distance is a scalar quantity (total path length covered).
    *   Displacement is a vector quantity (shortest straight-line distance from initial to final position).
    *   *Misconception Alert:* Displacement can be zero (e.g., returning to the start of a track), even if distance is positive. A non-zero average velocity cannot occur if displacement is zero.
*   **Newton's First Law (Inertia):**
    *   An object remains at rest or in uniform motion unless acted upon by a net external force.
    *   *Misconception Alert (Aristotelian Impetus Fallacy):* Continuous net force is NOT necessary to sustain a constant velocity. If $a = 0$ (constant velocity), then $F_{net} = 0$, even if the object is moving.
*   **Kinematic Equations (Uniform Acceleration):**
    1. $v = u + at$
    2. $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$
    3. $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$

### Chapter 10: Gravitation & Free Fall
*   **Universal Law of Gravitation:** $F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$
*   **Acceleration due to gravity ($g$):** $g = G \frac{M}{R^2}$. Note that $g$ depends only on the mass of the planet ($M$), not the mass of the falling object.
*   *Misconception Alert:* In a vacuum, all objects fall at the exact same rate regardless of their mass. A heavier object does NOT accelerate faster than a lighter object during free fall.

## GRADE 10 PHYSICS

### Chapter 10: Light - Reflection and Refraction
*   **Cartesian Sign Convention:**
    *   Pole ($P$) is the origin $(0,0)$.
    *   Object Distance ($u$) is always negative.
    *   **Concave Mirror:** Focal length ($f$) is always **Negative** (focus is in front).
    *   **Convex Mirror:** Focal length ($f$) is always **Positive** (focus is behind).
    *   *Misconception Alert:* Assigning $f = +15$ cm for a concave mirror is a critical error.
*   **Formulas:**
    *   Mirror Formula: $\frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{f}$
    *   Lens Formula: $\frac{1}{v} - \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{f}$
    *   *Misconception Alert:* Do not swap the + and - signs between mirrors and lenses.
*   **Image Formation by Concave Mirror:**
    *   *Misconception Alert:* A concave mirror does NOT only form real/inverted images. If the object is placed between the Pole ($P$) and Focus ($F$), it forms a **Virtual, Erect, and Enlarged** image behind the mirror.

### Chapter 12: Electricity and Circuits
*   **Current in Series:**
    *   *Misconception Alert (Current Attenuation Fallacy):* Electric current does NOT get "consumed" or "used up" as it passes through series resistors. The current is identically the same at all points in a series circuit.
*   **Current in Parallel:**
    *   *Misconception Alert:* Current does NOT split equally unless the parallel resistors are identical. Current divides inversely proportionally to resistance.
*   **Equivalent Resistance (Parallel):**
    *   $\frac{1}{R_p} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2}$
    *   *Misconception Alert:* After summing the fractions, you MUST take the reciprocal to find $R_p$.

## GRADE 11 PHYSICS

### Chapter 4: Motion in a Plane (2D Projectile Kinematics)
*   **Projectile Motion:**
    *   Horizontal velocity ($v_x = u \cos\theta$) remains constant.
    *   Vertical acceleration is always $a_y = -g$ (downwards).
    *   *Misconception Alert:* At the maximum height (apex), vertical velocity is zero ($v_y = 0$), but acceleration is STILL $a = -g$. Zero instantaneous velocity does NOT imply zero acceleration.

### Chapter 5: Laws of Motion (Dynamics & Inclined Planes)
*   **Normal Force on an Incline:**
    *   The Normal reaction force ($N$) is perpendicular to the surface.
    *   On an inclined plane of angle $\theta$, $N = mg \cos\theta$.
    *   *Misconception Alert:* Do not universally equate $N = mg$. This is only true on flat, horizontal surfaces with no other vertical forces acting.

## GRADE 12 PHYSICS

### Chapter 2: Electrostatics (Electric Potential & Fields)
*   **Potential ($V$) vs Field ($E$):**
    *   Electric Field is the negative gradient of Potential: $E = -\frac{dV}{dr}$
    *   *Misconception Alert:* Zero electric potential ($V = 0$) does NOT imply zero electric field ($E = 0$). For example, at the exact midpoint of an electric dipole, the potential $V = 0$, but the electric fields from the positive and negative charges point in the same direction and add up to a strong non-zero field.

### Chapter 6: Electromagnetic Induction (Lenz's Law)
*   **Faraday's Law:** $\varepsilon = -\frac{d\Phi}{dt}$
*   **Lenz's Law:** The direction of the induced current is such that it opposes the **change in magnetic flux** that produced it.
*   *Misconception Alert:* Lenz's law opposes the *change in flux*, not necessarily the external field itself. If an external field is decreasing (collapsing), the induced field will point in the SAME direction to "prop it up" and oppose the decrease.
